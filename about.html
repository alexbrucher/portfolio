About Alex

I see engineering as a practical way to serve human good. Climate work is where I most want to apply it: building things that address real needs while respecting cost, manufacturing capability, user behavior, and reliability.

My work has included adapting sheet-metal cookstoves, simplifying a buoy-release mechanism, building production jigs, and prototyping research hardware for kidney cryopreservation. The projects are different, but the useful skills carry across them: CAD, fabrication, assembly, testing, and learning enough about an unfamiliar field to contribute quickly.

Right now, I'm a master's student at Columbia, focused on energy systems. Outside of engineering, I spend my time hiking, camping, and cycling (I bikepacked over 1,000 km across Europe last summer).

I'm looking for an internship for Summer 2027. Whether you're looking for engineers or just want to connect, please reach out.
